Group Assignments

8 groups, generated to balance gender and rank while flagging relationship, family, community, and income conflicts.

Grouping Rules

These are the six criteria the auto-assignment engine applies, in priority order.

#RuleHow it's enforced
1Ranking (Test, IQ, Essay)Composite score is averaged, then top students are spread across all 8 groups using a snake draft — never clustered.
2Socio-economic statusStudents who report the exact same family income (e.g. two ₱100,000 entries) are automatically kept apart.
3GenderBoys and girls are each snake-drafted separately so every group gets a near-equal split.
4CommunityStudents sharing the same home community are spread apart where possible; remaining overlaps are flagged with a note.
5RelationshipsCouples (if reported to coordinators) are always placed in different groups.
6Family membersLinked family members are always placed in different groups.
