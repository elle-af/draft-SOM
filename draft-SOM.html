How this works

1. A Level Coordinator adds each student along with their ranking, family income, home community, and any known relationship or family links.

2. Once the roster is ready, click Generate Groups — the system builds 8 balanced groups automatically, following the school's grouping criteria.

3. Everyone (Dr. LG, Facilitators, Staff) can view the final groups. Level Coordinators can fine-tune by moving individual students between groups if needed.

4. These groups are meant to stay fixed for all 4 years, so review the results carefully before treating them as final.

Grouping criteria applied

🏅Ranking — top-ranked students (test, IQ, essay) are spread across groups, not clustered.
💰Socio-economic status — students with matching family income figures are kept apart.
⚧Gender — boys are spread as evenly as possible across all 8 groups.
🏘️Community — a group is never assigned duty in the home community of one of its own members.
💞Relationships — known couples are always placed in different groups.
👪Family — siblings / relatives are always placed in different groups.
